# Warranty & coverage: what BMW still pays for on an aging G20

**Applies to:** 2022 330i / 330i xDrive (terms verified from the MY2022 booklet). Other G20 years
are similar but check that year's booklet. Lists change.

> **TL;DR / ELI5:** The famous BMW coverage (4 yr / 50k bumper-to-bumper, 3 yr / 36k free
> maintenance) runs out early. **But emissions warranties run much longer, and they cover more
> than you'd think.** If the car is registered in one of 14 "California-rules" states (incl. PA,
> NY, NJ, MA, MD, CT, WA, OR, CO), BMW's own booklet covers a list of engine and transmission parts,
> including the **valve cover gasket, timing chain, turbo, transmission mechatronic, torque
> converter, injectors and high-pressure fuel pump**, for **7 years / 70,000 miles**. Every
> state gets **8 yr / 80k** on the catalytic converter and engine computer. So before ~70k miles,
> any oil seep at the valve cover or turbo/transmission weirdness should go to a **BMW dealer as a
> warranty claim**, not to an indie shop on your dime.

## Coverage at a glance (MY2022, from BMW NA's booklet)
| Coverage | Term (whichever first) | What it covers | Where |
|---|---|---|---|
| New Vehicle Limited Warranty | **4 yr / 50,000 mi** | Defects, nearly everything except wear items | BMW dealer |
| BMW Ultimate Care (scheduled maintenance) | **3 yr / 36,000 mi** | Factory-scheduled items: oil service, brake fluid, filters, plugs, vehicle check (per BMW's Maintenance Book) | BMW dealer |
| Rust perforation | 12 yr / unlimited | Rust-through from inside | BMW dealer |
| Federal emissions, general | 2 yr / 24,000 mi | Emission-related defects | BMW dealer |
| **Federal emissions, specific parts** | **8 yr / 80,000 mi** | **Catalytic converter; engine control module (DME) incl. on-board diagnostics** | BMW dealer |
| California emissions, general *(14 states)* | 3 yr / 50,000 mi | Any part that affects emissions | BMW dealer |
| **California emissions, listed parts** *(14 states)* | **7 yr / 70,000 mi** | **See list below** | BMW dealer |

Clocks start at the car's **original in-service date** (first retail sale, or first use as a
loaner/demo). Coverage **transfers to later owners**. Find the in-service date on the original
bill of sale, or ask any BMW service desk to look it up by VIN.

### The 14 "California emissions warranty" states (2022 booklet)
California, Colorado, Connecticut, Delaware, Maine, Maryland, Massachusetts, New Jersey, New York,
Oregon, **Pennsylvania**, Rhode Island, Vermont, Washington. It's based on where the car is
**registered**.

### 7 yr / 70k California-list parts for the **330i / 330i xDrive** (2022 booklet, pp. 21–23)
| System | Parts on the list |
|---|---|
| **Engine (mechanical)** | **Valve cover gaskets** (cylinder-head-cover profile seal) · **Timing chain** · Camshaft position adjustment unit · VANOS central valve · Valvetronic actuator |
| **Turbo / air** | **Turbocharger** · Turbo wastegate actuator · Charge-air duct (turbo → throttle) · Throttle body · Intake manifold w/ charge-air cooler · Intake manifold gasket · Exhaust manifold gasket (cylinder head) |
| **Fuel** | **Fuel injectors** · **High-pressure fuel pump** · Fuel rail · Fuel pump (in-tank) · Fuel tank · Fuel filler pipe · Fuel line (underbody rear) · EVAP canister |
| **Cooling / heat** | **Heat management module** · Cylinder-head and engine-block temperature sensors |
| **Transmission** | **Transmission control module (mechatronic)** · **Torque converter** |
| **Electronics / sensors** | Engine control module (DME) · Brake system control module (DSC) · Knock sensor · Rear O2 sensor · MIL (check-engine light) |
| **Emissions** | Catalytic converter · **PCV valve** |

**Note on the thermostat:** on the B46/B48 the map-controlled thermostat lives inside the **heat
management module**, which *is* on the 330i list. (The separate "Thermostat" line in the booklet is
for the M550i's V8.)

**Not on the list (you pay):** oil filter housing / its gaskets and coolant bushing (see
[`/known-issues/oil-filter-housing-leaks.md`](../known-issues/oil-filter-housing-leaks.md)), mechanical water pump,
coolant hoses / expansion tank, suspension, brakes, starter (unless recalled).

## How to actually use the emissions warranty
1. **Know your in-service date and mileage.** Under 70k and inside 7 years → listed parts are covered.
2. **Go to a BMW dealer** (warranty work only happens there). Describe the symptom (oil smell,
   seep on the valve cover, check-engine light, rough shifting).
3. If they find a listed part failed, **ask them to file it under the emissions warranty**
   and name the booklet section ("California Emission Warranty Parts List"). Advisors don't
   always think of it at 60k+ miles.
4. Under CA-rules warranty, the dealer must **approve or deny within 30 days, and in writing**
   if denied (booklet p. 17). BMW NA Customer Relations: 1-800-831-1117.
5. **Maintenance records matter.** BMW can deny if lack of maintenance caused the failure. Keep
   receipts, including DIY oil-change parts receipts.

## Using an independent shop doesn't void anything
Federal law (Magnuson-Moss) and the booklet itself: you can have maintenance done anywhere, with
non-BMW parts, as long as they're equivalent and you keep records. BMW can only deny a claim if
the non-BMW part or work **caused** the failure. (Booklet p. 10: "does not invalidate the
warranty on other components, unless non-genuine … parts cause damage to warranted parts.")

## Extended coverage that's easy to miss
| Item | Term | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **EVAP purge valve** (BMW SIB 01 03 24) | **15 yr / 150,000 mi**, transferable | Symptoms: hard start, rough idle, metallic ticking, check-engine light (fault codes 118001, 118401, 190F08, 191C02, 195014). The 2024 revision listed 2019 G20s; a 2026 revision reportedly covers 330i 2019–2024. ⚠️ **Ask a dealer to check the VIN's "Vehicle Comments" in BMW's warranty system (WVI)** |
| BMW **Value Service** pricing | Cars **37+ months** old | Not a warranty but a national dealer price program (≈94% of BMW centers): **oil service $119.99** for 4- and 6-cyl (2026). See [`/where-to-service/`](../where-to-service/README.md) |
| Goodwill | Case-by-case | If a known issue (e.g. oil filter housing coolant leak) shows up just after coverage ends, ask the dealer to request **goodwill** from BMW NA. Service history at BMW helps |

## Things that are always free regardless of age
- **Recalls** (see [`/recalls/`](../recalls/README.md)). No expiry, any owner.

## Certified Pre-Owned (CPO)
If the car was sold as **BMW Certified**, the CPO limited warranty adds **1 year / unlimited miles
after the 4yr/50k ends** (≈5 years from in-service total), with roadside assistance and
loaner/alternate transportation. CPO covers the oil-filter-housing coolant-leak repair (SIB 11 10 25).
Check your CPO paperwork for the exact end date. Older certification programs differed.
