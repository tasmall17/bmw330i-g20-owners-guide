# Upsell watch-list: legit vs. fluff on a G20 330i

**Applies to:** G20 330i / 330i xDrive (B46/B48, direct injection, electric power steering)

> **TL;DR / ELI5:** Most of what a BMW dealer recommends is real. The car's own maintenance
> computer (CBS) asks for it. The money leaks are (1) **dealer labor on 10-minute jobs**, (2)
> **"services" BMW never scheduled** (fuel/induction cleaning, engine flush, nitrogen, A/C
> "deodorizing", power-steering flush on a car with no power-steering fluid), and (3) **prepaid
> plans you won't use enough**. Say yes to the real items, ask for Value Service pricing, and do
> the trivial stuff yourself.

## Legit (do it, but watch the price)
| Item | Verdict | Fair price / note |
|---|---|---|
| Brake fluid (every 2 yrs, time-based) | **Legit** | $100–200; >$250 is high |
| Spark plugs (~60k) | **Legit** | $195–325 indie; dealer Value Service from ~$169 |
| Cabin microfilter (~20k / 2 yrs) | Legit, **DIY it** | Part $27–85; dealer often bills ~0.6 h for a 10-min job |
| Engine air filter (~40k) | Legit, **DIY it** | Part ~$27–42 |
| Transmission / transfer case / diff fluids at 50–80k | **Legit preventive**, even though BMW says "lifetime" | Indie BMW specialist; no flush machines |
| Tires when ≤ 4/32" and all four (xDrive) | **Legit** | 4–5/32" is "plan replacement," not "today" |
| Alignment after new tires | **Situational** | Yes with uneven wear/pull/suspension work; otherwise optional |
| Battery replacement at 4–6 yrs | **Situational** | Ask for the **test printout**; AGMs often last 5–7 yrs |
| Wiper blades | Legit, overpriced | DIY $20–75 |

## Fluff (decline)
| Item | Why |
|---|---|
| **Fuel induction / throttle-body / "fuel system service"** (BG 44K, EPR, MOA…) | Not in BMW's schedule. On a **direct-injection** engine fuel never touches the intake valves, so bottles can't clean them. BMW's booklet calls fuel additives not required. Use Top Tier fuel |
| **Engine flush** | Not BMW-approved; LL-17 FE+ oil already has detergents. Small risk, no benefit |
| **Nitrogen tire fill** | Marginal benefit; checking pressure monthly matters more |
| **Coolant flush at 4–5 yrs** | BMW HT-12 is "long-term." Do it only with a cooling repair |
| **A/C recharge with no diagnosed leak**, A/C "deodorizing/disinfection" | Low refrigerant = a leak to find. A musty smell is usually the cabin filter |
| **Power-steering flush** | G20 steering is electric. **There is no fluid** |
| **Fuel filter** | Lifetime, in-tank; BMW excludes it from maintenance |
| "Brake system service" / caliper clean-and-lube as a separate line | Part of any proper pad job |
| Alignment at every visit / every rotation | Only when needed |

## Prepaid maintenance plans (dealer oil plans, "Ultimate Care+", service contracts)
These can be fine *or* a waste. It's just math:
1. **Find the per-visit price you'd pay anyway.** For a G20 oil service at a dealer offering Value
   Service: **$119.99** (2026).
2. **Count the visits the plan covers that you'll actually use.** CBS oil interval ≈ 10k mi / 1 yr.
   Visits ≈ max(years, miles ÷ 10,000).
3. **Break-even = plan price ÷ per-visit price.** E.g., a ~$249 3-year oil plan ÷ $119.99 ≈ **2.1
   visits → pays off at the 3rd dealer oil service**. Someone driving ~12–15k mi/yr would use ~4 in
   3 years and come out ~$200+ ahead; someone selling the car next year loses.
4. Check: transferable on sale? refundable? what's excluded (top-offs, filters, fees)? Does it lock
   you into one dealer?

## How to say no without a fight
- "Can you show me where that's in BMW's maintenance schedule / CBS?"
- "Please price that under **Value Service**."
- "I'll hold off on that one today. Can you note the measurement (mm / 32nds / test result)?"
- "Please itemize parts with part numbers and labor hours."
