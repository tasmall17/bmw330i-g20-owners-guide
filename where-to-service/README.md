# Where to service it: dealer vs. independent shop vs. DIY

**Applies to:** G20 330i / 330i xDrive out of (or near the end of) factory coverage

> **TL;DR / ELI5:** Use the **dealer** for anything that's **free** (recalls, warranty, emissions
> warranty claims, CPO) and for the oil service if they offer BMW's **$119.99 Value Service**
> price. Use an **independent BMW specialist** for bigger paid jobs (brakes, fluids, leaks, mounts):
> same parts and know-how, roughly **30–50% less**, mostly because labor runs ~$120–190/hr vs
> ~$170–260/hr. **DIY** the 10-minute stuff (cabin filter, engine air filter, wipers, fob
> battery): the dealer charges $120–260 for jobs that cost you $20–85 in parts.

## Labor rates (2025–2026, US)
| Who | Typical hourly rate | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| BMW dealer | **~$170–260/hr**; $250–300+ in high-cost metros | Bills BMW flat-rate units. Ask for an itemized invoice with labor op codes + hours |
| Independent BMW / European specialist | **~$120–190/hr** | Look for ISTA (BMW's diagnostic software), BMW-specific experience, OE-supplier parts |
| General chain shop | varies | Fine for tires/alignment with BMW specs; avoid for BMW-specific procedures (EPB service mode, battery registration, transmission fill) |

> Data point: one US BMW dealer quote (Oct 2026) implied **$215/hr**.

## BMW Value Service (dealer price program for older cars)
- For BMWs **37+ months** old, at ~94% of US BMW centers.
- 2026: **oil service $119.99** (4- and 6-cyl). Some dealers also advertise pads w/ sensor from
  **$256.99** and spark plugs from **$168.99**. Ask for Value Service pricing **by name** and
  compare with the dealer's website / My BMW app for your VIN.

## The verdict cheat-sheet
| Job | Go to | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Recalls, warranty, **emissions-warranty claims** (7/70 California-list parts, 8/80 cat/DME), EVAP 15/150, CPO, goodwill | **Dealer** | Free. Only dealers can do it |
| Oil + filter | **Dealer at Value Service ($119.99)**, else indie or DIY | National fixed price is competitive. DIY saves ~$40–60 but needs a CBS reset tool |
| Brake fluid (every 2 yrs) | **Indie**; dealer if ≤ ~$200 | Easy to overpay at a dealer |
| Cabin filter, engine air filter, wipers, key battery | **DIY** | 5–15 min each |
| Spark plugs (~60k) | **DIY or indie**; dealer only near Value Service pricing | One special socket |
| Brakes (pads + rotors) | **Indie** (or DIY w/ scan tool for rear EPB) | $400–600/axle labor gap |
| Battery (AGM) | **Indie or DIY** + registration | Dealer $550–850 for a ~$250 part |
| Tires + alignment | **Tire retailer / indie** with BMW specs | See [`/tires/buying-and-installing.md`](../tires/buying-and-installing.md) |
| Transmission + transfer case + diff fluids (~50–80k) | **Indie BMW specialist** | Temperature-controlled fill; no flush machines |
| Leaks, mounts, bushings (out of coverage) | **Indie BMW specialist** | Roughly half the dealer price. **Check emissions warranty / CPO / goodwill first** |
| Diagnostics, software, driver-assist camera calibration | **Dealer** or an indie with ISTA | Needs BMW tooling |

## Myth: "Using an indie voids my warranty"
No. Federal law (Magnuson-Moss) and BMW's own warranty booklet: maintenance can be done anywhere,
with equivalent parts, if you keep records. See [`/vehicle/warranty-and-coverage.md`](../vehicle/warranty-and-coverage.md).
