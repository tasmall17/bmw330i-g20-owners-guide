# Coolant hoses & expansion tank (plastic vent hose quick-connects)

**Applies to:** 2019–2025 G20 330i & 330i xDrive (B46D/B48) · **Category:** known issue

> **TL;DR / ELI5:** The cooling system uses a lot of plastic: the expansion tank, small vent
> hoses, and quick-connect fittings that snap together. After years of heat cycles the plastic gets
> brittle, and a fitting or hose end can **crack or snap**, sometimes slowly (a seep), sometimes all
> at once (sudden coolant loss). It's a cheap part, but **driving on a low-coolant warning** is how
> a $300 fix becomes an overheated engine. If you see the coolant warning, a puddle, or sweet-smelling
> steam, stop and check.

## How much it actually matters: **4/5**
The part is cheap, but the consequences of ignoring it are not. Overheating can warp the head or
damage the head gasket.

## What the forums say
- **"Fix it when it happens" camp:** These are inexpensive plastic parts, and BMW sets no
  replacement interval. Watch the coolant level and fix leaks when they appear. ⚠️ Owner-thread
  detail couldn't be fetched; based on maintenance research summaries.
- **"Preventive at age/mileage" camp:** Some owners replace brittle plastic vent hoses and the
  expansion tank proactively at 60–80k or during other cooling work, since the fittings fail with
  age. ⚠️ unverified for the G20 specifically.
- **Where consensus lands:** Inspect hoses and fittings at every service, especially from about
  60k. Fix seeps right away, and replace adjacent brittle plastic while you're in there.

## When it shows up / when to act
| Source | Guidance |
|---|---|
| BMW position | Coolant is "long-term" (lifetime); hoses are replaced on failure only |
| Enthusiast view | Plastic vent hoses and expansion-tank fittings commonly fail around **60–80k** |
| Signs it's happening now | Low-coolant Check Control message, sweet smell, pink/green crust at a fitting, a damp spot under the front of the car, steam |

## DIY verdict: **Doable with tools**
- **You need:** the specific hose or tank (look it up by VIN in RealOEM/ETK), **BMW HT-12** coolant
  (83 19 2 468 442) mixed 50/50 with distilled water, and new clips/O-rings.
- **The gotcha:** The **bleed procedure**. BMW's routine runs the electric pump through a bleed
  cycle (commonly: ignition on, engine off, heater on max, hold the accelerator about 10 s ⚠️
  verify). Do a second bleed after the first heat cycle, and **never open the cap hot**.

## Watch out for
- **Warranty angle:** Coolant hoses and the expansion tank are **not** on the emissions warranty
  lists. The [heat management module (thermostat)](heat-management-module-thermostat.md) **is**,
  for 7 yr / 70k in California-list states. If the leak is at the thermostat housing, that's a
  different and possibly free repair.
- Coolant near the oil filter is a different known issue: the
  [oil filter housing coolant bushing](oil-filter-housing-leaks.md) (BMW SIB 11 10 25).
- Don't mix HT-12 with older blue BMW coolant without a full flush.
- A "coolant flush" sold *without* a leak repair is generally an upsell on this car. See
  [`../maintenance/`](../maintenance/README.md).
