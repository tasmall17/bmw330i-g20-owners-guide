# Front control arm & thrust arm (tension strut) bushings

**Applies to:** 2019–2025 G20 330i & 330i xDrive · **Category:** known issue

> **TL;DR / ELI5:** Each front wheel is held in place by metal arms that pivot on rubber
> **bushings**. The big one at the front of each wheel well, often called the **thrust arm** or
> tension strut, takes braking forces. As the rubber wears or cracks you get **clunks over bumps or
> when braking, a creak when parking, or wandering/vague steering**, and the tires can wear
> unevenly. It's a normal wear item around 50–70k. Replace in **left/right pairs** and get an
> **alignment** afterward.

## How much it actually matters: **3/5**
Worn bushings don't stop the car, but they hurt steering precision, braking stability, and tire
life, which matters on expensive tires and on xDrive, where even tire wear matters.

## What the forums say
- **"Normal wear, fix on symptoms" camp (consensus):** Front-end bushings are a mid-life BMW wear
  item. Replace when the clunks and wandering show up. ⚠️ G20 threads couldn't be fetched;
  summarized from maintenance research and RepairPal's known-problem listing.
- **Cold-weather creaks:** bimmer-service.com notes G20 "creaks or knocks in cold weather" from
  worn **stabilizer links or bushings**. Rule those cheaper parts out first.
- **Where consensus lands:** Have a shop confirm the play with the car on a lift. Replace in pairs,
  then align.

## When it shows up / when to act
| Source | Guidance |
|---|---|
| BMW position | No interval. Replace on inspection failure |
| Enthusiast view | Moderately common at **50–70k** |
| Signs it's happening now | Clunk over bumps or under braking, creak when turning at parking speeds, steering wheel shimmy under braking, vague on-center steering, inner/outer tire edge wear |

## DIY verdict: **Doable with tools** (intermediate)
- **You need:** both arms (OEM or quality OE like Lemförder), new single-use bolts/nuts as BMW
  specifies, a torque wrench, and a ride-height reference.
- **The gotcha:** Bushing bolts must be **final-torqued at ride height** (car's weight on the
  wheels), or the new bushings are pre-twisted and wear fast. Then **align**.

## Watch out for
- **Warranty angle:** suspension isn't on the emissions lists. Only the new-car warranty or CPO
  covers it.
- Make sure it isn't the [steering creak / tie-rod issue](steering-creak-tie-rods.md) (BMW
  SIB 32 01 23), which makes a similar noise when turning at a standstill.
- An alignment **without** fixing worn bushings is wasted money.
- If one side failed, the other is usually close. Pairs make sense here.
