# Heat management module (thermostat)

**Applies to:** 2019–2025 G20 330i & 330i xDrive (B46D/B48) · **Category:** known issue

> **TL;DR / ELI5:** On these engines the thermostat isn't a simple $20 valve. It sits inside a
> computer-controlled plastic housing BMW calls the **heat management module**, which decides where
> coolant flows. It can leak at the housing or stick, so the engine runs too hot or too cold. The good
> news: in 14 states (CA, CO, CT, DE, ME, MD, MA, NJ, NY, OR, PA, RI, VT, WA), BMW lists the **heat
> management module** on its **7 year / 70,000 mile** emissions warranty for the 330i. Inside that
> window, take it to a BMW dealer and ask for it to be covered.

## How much it actually matters: **4/5**
A stuck or leaking thermostat can overheat the engine (expensive), or keep it cold (poor heat,
fuel economy, and emissions). It's also often free inside 7 yr / 70k in California-list states.

## What the forums say
- **Common view:** The plastic thermostat/housing is a known 60k+ failure point on BMW
  four-cylinders. Owners usually find it through a coolant-temperature fault or a seep at the
  housing. ⚠️ G20 forum threads couldn't be fetched; summarized from maintenance research and
  parts-seller guides.
- **"Do it preventively" camp:** Some replace it alongside other cooling-system work after 60–80k.
- **Where consensus lands:** Replace on symptoms, not on a schedule. Check warranty coverage first.

## When it shows up / when to act
| Source | Guidance |
|---|---|
| BMW position | No interval. **"Heat Management Module" is on the California Emission Warranty Parts List** (7 yr / 70k) for the 330i. (The separate "Thermostat" line in that list applies to the M550i's V8, not the 330i.) |
| Enthusiast view | Moderately common from about **60k** |
| Signs it's happening now | Coolant-temperature fault codes or a check-engine light; temperature running high or slow to warm up; weak cabin heat; coolant seep or crust at the thermostat housing |

## DIY verdict: **Doable with tools** (intermediate)
- **You need:** the heat management module for your VIN (RealOEM/ETK), new seals, BMW HT-12
  coolant, and ideally a scan tool to run the bleed and clear codes.
- **The gotcha:** The cooling-system **bleed** and electrical connectors made brittle by heat.

## Watch out for
- **Emissions warranty first:** under 7 yr / 70k in a California-list state, ask a BMW dealer to
  file it under the **California Emission Control System Limited Warranty**. See
  [`../vehicle/warranty-and-coverage.md`](../vehicle/warranty-and-coverage.md).
- **A leak near the thermostat might be something else:** the
  [oil filter housing coolant bushing](oil-filter-housing-leaks.md) or a
  [plastic vent hose](coolant-hoses-and-expansion-tank.md). Have the shop pinpoint the source.
- Don't keep driving on a low-coolant warning or a rising temperature gauge.
