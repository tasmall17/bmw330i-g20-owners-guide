# Oil filter housing leaks (coolant and oil)

**Applies to:** 2019–2025 G20 330i & 330i xDrive (B46D), plus other B46/B48 four-cylinder BMWs · **Category:** known issue

> **TL;DR / ELI5:** The oil filter housing is a plastic-and-aluminum block on the engine that
> holds the oil filter. Oil **and coolant** both pass through it, sealed by gaskets and a small
> coolant bushing (insert). On these engines the seal can squeeze that bushing until it cracks,
> which makes a **coolant leak**. BMW admitted this in a December 2025 bulletin and redesigned
> the part. Separately, the oil gaskets harden with age and seep **oil**. Signs: sweet coolant
> smell, a low-coolant warning, crusty residue or wetness around the housing, or a burning-oil
> smell. Fix it at the first real seep, and **replace the coolant bushing and the oil gaskets in
> one job**, because the labor (the intake comes off) is most of the bill.

## How much it actually matters: **4/5**
A seep is a moderate repair. Left alone, coolant loss can overheat the engine, and oil dripping
onto the belt or exhaust can wreck the belt or start a fire. Both turn a ~$1,000 job into a much
bigger one.

## What the forums say
- **"Wait for the leak" camp (the consensus):** Inspect at every oil change and fix at the first
  real seep, before oil reaches the belt or exhaust. There's no reason to replace dry gaskets
  early. ⚠️ Summarized from forum search results; the threads themselves couldn't be fetched.
- **"Do it preventively" camp:** Mostly parts sellers. Euro Premium Parts reports seepage
  starting around 40–70k miles on the B46. An aftermarket **aluminum** housing for the G20 330i
  (RhineWerks) exists because people worry about the plastic original.
- **Where consensus lands:** Check it at every service. When it's wet, do the full reseal: new
  gasket set plus the redesigned coolant bushing.

## When it shows up / when to act
| Source | Guidance |
|---|---|
| BMW position | No scheduled replacement. **SIB 11 10 25** (2025-12-12): coolant leaks happen because "the seal for the oil filter housing could exert excessive pressure to the bushing/insert". Fix is gaskets plus a **redesigned** bush |
| Enthusiast view | Common at **40–100k**. Inspect at every oil change. Fix at the first seep |
| Signs it's happening now | Sweet smell after driving, low-coolant warning, white/green crust near the filter housing, oil wetness on the housing or lower down, burning-oil smell |

## DIY verdict: **Leave it to a shop** (for most owners)
- **You need:** gasket set **11 42 9 886 567**, bush **11 42 8 490 951**, a new oil filter
  element, BMW HT-12 coolant, approved oil. Confirm part numbers for your VIN in ETK/RealOEM.
- **The gotcha:** Access. The intake comes off, and the cooling system has to be refilled and
  bled afterward (a scan tool or the bleed routine).

## Watch out for
- **Warranty angle:** BMW's bulletin says the repair is covered under the **New Vehicle Limited
  Warranty (4 yr / 50k) or BMW CPO** only. If you're just past either, ask the dealer to request
  **goodwill** from BMW NA. A BMW service history helps.
- The oil filter housing is **not** on the California-list 7 yr / 70k emissions warranty. See
  [`../vehicle/warranty-and-coverage.md`](../vehicle/warranty-and-coverage.md).
- **Class action:** *Eiger v. BMW NA* (D.N.J., filed Feb 16 2026) names B46/B48/B58 engines in
  **2014–2021** models. As filed, it doesn't include MY2022+. ⚠️ Status not tracked.
- **Wetness elsewhere could be this leak.** Oil from here can run down onto lower parts
  (engine support arm, mounts, oil pan) and look like *their* leak. Ask where the oil actually
  starts. See [`engine-mounts.md`](engine-mounts.md).
- Insist on the **redesigned** bush, not only new gaskets.
