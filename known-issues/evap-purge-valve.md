# EVAP purge (vent) valve: 15 yr / 150k extended warranty

**Applies to:** G20 330i & 330i xDrive (B46D). The 2024 bulletin revision lists **MY2019**; eligibility
is decided **per VIN** · **Category:** known issue

> **TL;DR / ELI5:** The EVAP purge valve lets fuel vapor from the gas tank's charcoal canister get
> burned in the engine instead of escaping. When it fails you can get a **hard start after refueling,
> rough idle, a metallic ticking, or a check-engine light**. BMW extended the warranty on this valve
> to **15 years / 150,000 miles** for affected cars. Before paying anyone to replace it, ask a BMW
> dealer to check your VIN's warranty notes. It may be **free**.

## How much it actually matters: **3/5**
Driveability and check-engine light nuisance, plus failed emissions inspections in states that
test. It's only moderately important mechanically, but very important to check before paying.

## What the forums say
- ⚠️ Not researched in forums. This doc is based on BMW's own bulletin.
- The bulletin itself warns that BMW's notification letters "may not have been correctly
  assigned" to VINs, so **don't rely on whether you got a letter.** Ask for the VIN check.

## When it shows up / when to act
| Source | Guidance |
|---|---|
| BMW position | **SIB 01 03 24** (rev. 1, 2024-09-12): extended limited warranty on the EVAP purge (vent) valve to **15 years / 150,000 miles** from original in-service date. Eligible VINs show this "Vehicle Comment" in BMW's Warranty Vehicle Inquiry (WVI): *SI B01 03 24 (RC 13 90 90 03 00)*. A later revision reportedly broadens it to 330i 2019–2024 ⚠️ unverified |
| Enthusiast view | Get the VIN checked as soon as you see symptoms or codes |
| Signs it's happening now | Hard start (often right after refueling), rough idle, metallic ticking from the engine bay, check-engine light. Fault codes cited: **118001, 118401, 190F08, 191C02, 195014** |

## DIY verdict: **Leave it to a shop** (a BMW dealer, because of the warranty)
- **You need:** the VIN check first. Only if you're not covered: the correct valve by VIN and a
  scan tool to clear and verify codes.
- **The gotcha:** Paying an indie for a part BMW would have replaced for free.

## Watch out for
- **Ask the dealer exactly this:** *"Please check WVI Vehicle Comments for SI B01 03 24, the EVAP
  purge valve 15/150 extended warranty."*
- The bulletin's model list is "for informational purposes only, it is not the only deciding
  factor". A model year not appearing doesn't rule your car out.
- Coverage carries the same eligibility rules and exclusions as the New Vehicle Limited Warranty.
- Other EVAP parts (charcoal canister, fuel filler pipe) are on the **California-list 7 yr / 70k**
  emissions warranty in 14 states. See [`../vehicle/warranty-and-coverage.md`](../vehicle/warranty-and-coverage.md).
