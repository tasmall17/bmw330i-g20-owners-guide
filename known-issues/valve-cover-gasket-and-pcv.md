# Valve cover gasket & PCV (crankcase vent) valve

**Applies to:** 2019–2025 G20 330i & 330i xDrive (B46D/B48) · **Category:** known issue

> **TL;DR / ELI5:** The valve cover is the plastic lid on top of the engine. Its rubber gasket
> hardens over time and starts seeping oil, usually somewhere past 60k miles. On these engines the
> **PCV valve** (the crankcase ventilation valve, which vents engine fumes back into the intake) is
> built into that cover, so when the PCV fails (whistling, rough idle, burning oil) the fix is a new
> cover too. **Before you pay for this, check the emissions warranty.** In 14 states (CA, CO, CT,
> DE, ME, MD, MA, NJ, NY, OR, PA, RI, VT, WA), BMW lists **valve cover gaskets and the PCV valve**
> as covered for **7 years / 70,000 miles**. Plenty of owners pay $2,000+ for a repair BMW should
> have done for free.

## How much it actually matters: **4/5**
Oil seeping onto the hot exhaust smells and can be a fire risk. A failed PCV causes rough running
and oil consumption. It's moderately expensive out of pocket and **free** for many cars still
inside 7 yr / 70k.

## What the forums say
- **"Wait for the leak" camp (consensus):** Don't replace a dry valve cover preventively. Fix it at
  the first real seep or the first PCV symptom. ⚠️ Summarized from forum search results; G20
  threads couldn't be fetched.
- **"It will go eventually" camp:** Euro Premium Parts reports B46 valve cover seepage around
  **60–80k**. bimmer-service.com says the plastic cover and integrated gasket "are prone to hardening
  and leaking oil over high mileage."
- **Where consensus lands:** Inspect at oil changes. If it seeps **before 70k in a California-list
  state**, it's a warranty claim. After that, use an independent BMW shop.

| Source | Guidance |
|---|---|

## DIY verdict: **Doable with tools** (for an advanced DIYer)
- **You need:** a complete valve cover (comes with the integrated PCV and a new gasket), Torx
  bits, a torque wrench, and new injector/coil-area seals if called for.
- **The gotcha:** Plastic clips and connectors are brittle at this age. Torque the cover bolts in
  sequence to spec, because overtightening warps the plastic.

## Watch out for
- **Emissions warranty first.** If the car is registered in a California-list state and is
  under **7 years / 70k miles** from in-service, ask a BMW dealer to file it under the
  **California Emission Control System Limited Warranty**. The dealer must answer within 30 days,
  in writing if denied. Details in
  [`../vehicle/warranty-and-coverage.md`](../vehicle/warranty-and-coverage.md).
- **Federal-only states:** the valve cover isn't on the 8 yr / 80k list (that list covers only the
  catalytic converter and engine computer). Ask about **goodwill** if you're barely out of
  new-car warranty.
- **Oil found lower on the engine may be coming from here.** Oil from the cover or the
  [oil filter housing](oil-filter-housing-leaks.md) can run down onto mounts and support arms. Make
  the shop show you where the oil *starts*.
- If you're near 70k, have it inspected **before** the mileage runs out.
