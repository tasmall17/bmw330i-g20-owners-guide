# Steering creak/knock when turning (tie-rod thrust bearing)

**Applies to:** G20 330i & 330i xDrive fitted with a ThyssenKrupp (TKPS) electric steering rack.
G20s can have either TKPS or JTEKT racks, so it's car-specific. · **Category:** known issue

> **TL;DR / ELI5:** Some G20s make a **cracking, creaking, or knocking** noise from the front when
> you turn the steering wheel **while parked**, or when driving over rough roads. BMW traced it to a
> bearing inside the **tie rod** (the rod linking the steering rack to each wheel) that's too stiff
> to turn smoothly. The fix is new tie rods plus an alignment check. It's an **annoying noise, not a
> safety failure**, and BMW covers it only under the new-car warranty. Out of warranty, decide
> whether the noise bothers you enough to pay.

## How much it actually matters: **2/5**
It's mostly a noise and refinement issue. Don't confuse it with recall **20V355000** (2019–2020
330i xDrive / M340i tie rods that could *fracture*). That one *is* safety-critical and free. See
[`../recalls/README.md`](../recalls/README.md).

## What the forums say
- ⚠️ G20 threads couldn't be fetched. Research summaries note that one revision of the bulletin
  told dealers **not to replace parts on G20s until updated parts were released**. The current parts
  status is unverified.
- **Where consensus lands:** If it's the noise in BMW's sample videos and you're under warranty,
  have the dealer fix it under SIB 32 01 23. Out of warranty, it's optional.

## When it shows up / when to act
| Source | Guidance |
|---|---|
| BMW position | **SIB 32 01 23** (2023-03-08). Cause: "breakaway torque in the thrust bearing of the tie rod is too high." Fix: replace one or both tie rods, then do an alignment check with a ride-height measurement. G20s built up to 04/2019 always get both. The dealer must first confirm a TKPS rack via ISTA |
| Enthusiast view | Fix if it bothers you; it isn't a wear-out danger |
| Signs it's happening now | Creak or knock when turning the wheel at a standstill; cracking over uneven roads |

## DIY verdict: **Leave it to a shop**
- **You need:** confirmation of which rack is fitted (TKPS vs JTEKT, via ISTA), the correct tie rods
  by VIN, and an alignment.
- **The gotcha:** Diagnosis. Worn [thrust-arm bushings](control-arm-and-thrust-arm-bushings.md) or
  stabilizer links make similar noises. Rule those out first.

## Watch out for
- **Warranty angle:** covered only under the **New Vehicle Limited Warranty** per the SIB. Not on
  the emissions lists.
- **Recall check:** 2019–2020 330i xDrive owners should confirm recall **20V355000** (tie-rod
  fracture) was done.
- Ask the shop to play BMW's example sound videos (V32 01 23 / V32 02 23) and confirm yours matches.
