# Weak cabin airflow / outside smells after a cabin filter change (recirc flap)

**Applies to:** G20 330i & 330i xDrive (BMW SIB 64 03 21 lists the G20) · **Category:** known issue

> **TL;DR / ELI5:** Changing the cabin air filter is a 10-minute DIY job, with one trap. Inside the
> housing there's a small flap that switches between fresh air and recirculated air. If it's in the
> wrong position while you slide the new filter in, the filter can **push the flap past its stop**.
> Afterward you get **weak airflow and outside smells** coming in. Fixing it means pulling the
> blower motor, which takes hours. The prevention is simple: **turn recirculation OFF** (fresh-air
> mode, LED off) before you insert the new filter, as BMW's bulletin says.

## How much it actually matters: **2/5**
It's a comfort issue, not a mechanical one. But the fix costs far more than the filter job that
caused it, so knowing about it is worth a lot.

## What the forums say
- **Conflicting advice exists.** A popular Bimmerpost "Warning!!!" thread tells owners to turn
  recirculation **ON**. **BMW's bulletin says OFF.** Follow the bulletin. ⚠️ Forum thread seen via
  search summary only.
- An independent shop's write-up describes the same failure on 2019–2025 G20s and confirms the SIB
  repair.
- **Where consensus lands:** It's an easy DIY. Just set recirculation **OFF** (fresh air) first.

## When it shows up / when to act
| Source | Guidance |
|---|---|
| BMW position | **SIB 64 03 21** (Jun 2021): the fresh-air/recirculation flap can be pushed past its end stop during the filter swap. Insert the filter with recirculation **OFF** |
| Enthusiast view | Same. Mind the flap during every cabin filter change |
| Signs it's happening now | Right after a filter change: noticeably weaker airflow, outside or exhaust smells with recirc on, recirc seems to "not work" |

## DIY verdict: **Dead simple** (the filter job), as long as you follow the one rule
- **You need:** cabin filter **64 11 9 382 886** (activated charcoal; Mann/Valeo equivalents fine).
  The G20 has **one** filter, in the passenger footwell behind the under-glovebox cover.
- **The gotcha:** Recirculation **OFF** before inserting the filter. See
  [`../maintenance/`](../maintenance/README.md) for the full filter job.

## Watch out for
- If a shop or dealer changed your filter and the airflow was weak afterward, **go back**. It's
  their workmanship issue.
- A dealer cabin-filter line item often bills ~0.6 h of labor for a 10-minute job. DIY is the
  money-saver here.
