# Known issues: G20 330i / 330i xDrive (B46D/B48)

Things that **break** (not scheduled maintenance) on these cars, ranked roughly by how likely you
are to meet them × what they cost. Every doc covers what it is in plain English, how much it
matters, the forum spectrum, DIY / indie / dealer cost and time, and **warranty angles first**,
because several of these may be free.

## Start here
1. **[starter-recall.md](starter-recall.md)**: 2021–2024 330i fire-risk recall. Free. **Check your VIN today.**
2. **Before paying for any oil/coolant/engine repair, check
   [`../vehicle/warranty-and-coverage.md`](../vehicle/warranty-and-coverage.md).** In 14 states the valve cover
   gasket, PCV, heat management module (thermostat), turbo and more are covered **7 yr / 70k**.
3. **Wet engine? Find where the fluid *starts*.** Oil from the
   [valve cover](valve-cover-gasket-and-pcv.md) or [oil filter housing](oil-filter-housing-leaks.md)
   runs down onto [mounts](engine-mounts.md) and support arms, and different sources have different
   costs and warranty answers.

## Also seen (not yet given their own doc)
- **Mechanical water pump / electric auxiliary coolant pump** failures (less common at 40–70k).
  ⚠️ Pump type on the B46/B48 needs verification by VIN in RealOEM.
- **Oil pan seepage** (uncommon this early; RepairPal lists oil pan replacement at $2,028–2,587).
- **High-pressure fuel pump** (occasional at 50–80k; on the CA-list 7 yr / 70k).
- **Brake squeal / rear pads wearing faster than fronts.** See [`../maintenance/`](../maintenance/README.md).
- **iDrive glitches, parking sensor/camera faults.** Usually software or connectors.
- **SIB 41 07 25** ("cracking" noise from rear wheel arch, 330i 2020–2025) and **SIB 51 22 22** (hood
  stays at pre-latch). ⚠️ Details not retrieved.
