# Recalls: BMW 330i / 330i xDrive (G20, 2019–2025)

> **TL;DR / ELI5:** A recall is the manufacturer admitting a safety defect and promising to fix it
> **free, at any BMW dealer, forever** (recall repairs don't expire with the warranty, and you
> don't need to be the original owner). A model year being listed here does **not** mean *your*
> car is included. Only a VIN check tells you that. Do the check: it takes 30 seconds and the
> 2025–2026 starter recalls involve **fire risk**.

## How to check YOUR car (do this first)
1. **nhtsa.gov/recalls**: type your 17-character VIN. Shows open (unrepaired) recalls for that exact car.
2. **BMW**: bmwusa.com recall lookup, the My BMW app, or call BMW Customer Relations
   **1-800-525-7417**.
3. **At any service visit**, ask the advisor: *"Are there any open campaigns or recalls on this VIN?"*
   Dealers see open campaigns instantly. If one's open, they can often do it the same visit.

> NHTSA notes VINs for a new recall become searchable some weeks after the recall is filed. If you
> got a letter but the VIN search shows nothing, call BMW.

## Recall vs. TSB vs. "campaign" (what the service advisor means)
- **Recall:** safety defect, federally tracked, always free, no expiry.
- **Service campaign / customer-care program:** non-safety fix BMW does voluntarily (sometimes only
  while under warranty, sometimes extended). Ask: *"Any open campaigns, not just recalls?"*
- **TSB (Technical Service Bulletin):** a repair recipe BMW sends dealers for a known issue. Not
  free by itself. It's free only if you're under a warranty that covers the part. Knowing the TSB
  exists helps you get a faster, correct diagnosis.
