# Tire categories, explained for a 330i (what each one *feels* like)

**Applies to:** G20 330i / 330i xDrive, any wheel size

> **TL;DR / ELI5:** The category you pick changes the car more than the brand does. **Summer**
> tires make it feel sharpest and most "planted," but they're unsafe in cold and snow.
> **Ultra-high-performance (UHP) all-season** tires give you ~85% of that feel year-round, with good rain grip
> and OK light snow. **Grand-touring all-season** tires are the quietest and softest but dull the
> steering. **All-weather** tires (with the snowflake-on-mountain symbol) handle real snow but feel
> softer when you push. **Winter** tires are for a second set of wheels in snow country. xDrive
> helps you *go* in snow; only tires help you **stop and turn**.

## The categories side by side
| Category | How it feels on a 330i xDrive | Works when | Tread life | Noise | Snow |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Max-performance summer** (Michelin PS4S, Goodyear Eagle F1 Asymmetric 6, Continental ECS02, Bridgestone Potenza Sport) | Quickest, most linear steering. Car feels **locked down mid-corner**. Much shorter braking. Clean launches when warm. Firm edge over sharp bumps (still usually smoother than factory run-flats) | Above **~40–45°F**. Below ~20°F don't drive *or even move the car* (rubber can crack). Store above 20°F | ~30k warranty; UTQG 300–340 | Moderate | **None. Dangerous** |
| **UHP all-season** (Pirelli P Zero AS Plus 3, Continental DWS06 Plus, Michelin PS All Season 4, Goodyear Eagle F1 All Season) | ~80–90% of summer sharpness, a touch softer on-center. **Calm and composed in rain.** Gets moving fine in light snow with xDrive | Year-round where winters are moderate | 45–50k warranty | Low–moderate | Light snow only |
| **Grand-touring all-season** (Michelin Primacy Tour A/S, Pirelli P7 AS Plus 3) | Quietest and softest. Steering lighter, slower to react | Year-round | Longest | Lowest | Light snow |
| **All-weather, 3PMSF** (Michelin CrossClimate 2, Vredestein Quatrac Pro+) | Softer and a bit "squirmy" when pushed hard; **real snow capability** | Year-round, incl. severe snow | 50–60k | Low–moderate | **Good** (severe-snow rated) |
| **Winter: studless** (Michelin X-Ice Snow, Nokian Hakkapeliitta R5, Bridgestone Blizzak IcePeak) | Vague and soft on dry pavement, longer dry stops. **On snow/ice, xDrive on these feels unstoppable** | Below ~45°F only | Short if run in summer | Moderate | **Excellent** + ice |
| **Winter: performance** (Michelin Pilot Alpin 5, Pirelli Sottozero 3) | Sharper on dry/wet than studless, less ice grip | Below ~45°F | — | Moderate | Very good |

> **3PMSF** = "three-peak mountain snowflake" symbol on the sidewall: passed a standardized
> snow-traction test. "M+S" alone means almost nothing.

## Picking by climate (one-set owners)
| Your winters | Best category | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Rarely below 40°F | **Summer** | Max grip, no downside |
| Cold, occasional moderate snow; plowed roads (most of the Mid-Atlantic, lower Midwest) | **UHP all-season**, or **all-weather** if you can't stay home on snow days | xDrive + a good all-season handles moderate storms; all-weather adds real snow braking |
| Regular snow/ice, unplowed roads, hills | **All-weather** (one set) or **summer/UHP + winter set** (two sets) | Stopping and turning on snow need snow-rated rubber |

## "Hook" and "planted," translated
- **Hook (launch grip):** with xDrive, launches are rarely traction-limited on dry roads. Tire
  compound shows up as fewer traction-control interventions, mainly in rain and cold. Summer > UHP
  all-season > all-weather > winter (on dry).
- **Planted (mid-corner + highway stability):** stiffer tread blocks + a crisp sidewall. Summer >
  UHP all-season. **Dropping run-flats** can make the ride better *and* keep the car feeling
  planted. See [`run-flat-vs-non-run-flat.md`](run-flat-vs-non-run-flat.md).
