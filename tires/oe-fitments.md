# Factory wheel & tire fitments: 2022 330i / 330i xDrive (US)

**Applies to:** 2022 G20 330i / 330i xDrive (most also apply 2019–2022). Pre-LCI (2023+) cars added
other styles.

> **TL;DR / ELI5:** Before you shop, go look at your tires. The sidewall tells you the size (e.g.
> `225/45R18`), the load/speed code (`95H`), whether they're **run-flats** (`RFT`, `RSC`, `SSR`,
> `ZP`, `ROF`) and whether they're **BMW-approved** (a ★ star). Most 2022 330is came on either
> **18" square** (same size all four corners, rotatable) or **19" staggered** (wider rears, can't
> rotate front↔back). Which one you have changes the price, the choices, and the rules.

**Factory brands vary by build**: Pirelli, Bridgestone, Michelin, Continental all appear.

## Tire pressures
Use **your car's B-pillar placard and iDrive** (Vehicle status → Tire Pressure Monitor). iDrive stores
targets per tire type/size. Third-party figures for reference:
| Setup | Front / Rear (psi, normal load) |
|---|---|
| 225/45R18 square | 32 / 38–39 |
| 19" staggered | 35 / 38 |
| 225/40R19 square | 35 / 42 ⚠️ |

## The load-index trap (18")
Factory 225/45R18 is **95 XL** (extra load). Some popular tires in that size only come as **91 SL**
(e.g. Continental DWS06 Plus and ExtremeContact Sport 02 in 225/45R18). Tire Rack's fitment filter
for this car requires XL, and many shops will refuse to install a lower load index. **Buy a 95 XL
tire in 225/45R18.**

## The star (★) marking
★ = BMW asked for changes to the tire, tested it on the chassis, and approved it. It's **not
required**, and a non-star tire doesn't void anything. Star tires can feel closest to factory
(often tuned for comfort on non-M cars). Treat it as a tiebreaker, not a must.
