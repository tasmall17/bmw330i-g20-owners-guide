# Buying & installing tires: where, what it really costs, what to ask for

**Applies to:** G20 330i / 330i xDrive

> **TL;DR / ELI5:** The sticker price per tire is only part of it. Budget **$130–300 for
> installation** (mount, balance, valve/TPMS service, disposal) and **~$100–150 for an alignment**
> if the old tires wore unevenly. Online (Tire Rack → local installer) has the best selection;
> **Costco** bundles installation + road hazard and runs Michelin deals; **Discount Tire** price-
> matches. Buy all four (xDrive), and check for **manufacturer rebates**: fall and spring are rebate
> seasons.

| **Discount Tire / America's Tire** | Price-matches; good local service; free flat repairs/rotations on tires bought there | Excluded from some maker rebates (runs own promos) | Install ~$20–25/tire; TPMS service ~$15–20/wheel |
| **BMW dealer** | ★ tires easy; paperwork trail if an xDrive shudder claim ever comes up | Usually most expensive | Ask them to match Tire Rack's price |

## What to ask for at install
1. **All four, same model**, and confirm the **load index** (95 XL on 225/45R18).
2. **Check the DOT date code**: tires should be under ~1 year old (`2526` = 25th week of 2026).
3. **Torque wheels by hand** to spec (BMW: 140 Nm / ~103 lb-ft), not just an impact gun.
4. **TPMS**: no reprogramming needed for same sensors. After install, iDrive → Vehicle status →
   Tire Pressure Monitor → confirm tire type → **Perform reset**, then drive.
5. **Set pressures to the placard** for the new tire (iDrive's selected tire type matters).
6. **Alignment?** Yes if the old tires wore unevenly (inner edges = rear toe or camber), the car
   pulls, or the wheel's off-center. Otherwise optional. G20 rear camber isn't adjustable; rear toe is.
7. **Road-force balance** if you feel a vibration at 60–75 mph afterward (common ask on 19").
