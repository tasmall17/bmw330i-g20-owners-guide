# xDrive tire rules (the ones that protect a $1,500–4,000 transfer case)

**Applies to:** every xDrive (AWD) G20 330i / M340i, and BMW xDrive cars generally

> **TL;DR / ELI5:** xDrive sends power to both axles through a clutch pack in the transfer case.
> It assumes all four tires are the **same rolling size**. If one axle's tires are noticeably
> more worn (smaller) than the other's, the clutch is constantly slipping to make up the difference.
> That means heat, burnt fluid, and eventually a **shudder** and a big bill. BMW's own service bulletin
> lists "unevenly worn or incorrect fitment tires" as the first cause of xDrive shudder. So:
> **replace all four at once, same model and size, and rotate a square setup regularly.**

## The rules
1. **Replace all four together.** Same brand, model, and size pattern as specified for your car.
2. **Tread-depth difference:** keep all four within **2/32"** (Tire Rack's rule) or about **2 mm**
   (≈2.5/32", the commonly-quoted BMW figure, ⚠️ from forums; BMW Repair Instruction 3612 isn't
   public).
3. **One tire ruined early?** Buy one new tire and have it **shaved** to match the others. Tire Rack
   does this for about **$25–35/tire**. Cheaper than three extra tires, far cheaper than a transfer case.
4. **Never mix categories across axles** (summer front + all-season rear, or worn + new).
5. **Square setup (e.g. 18" 225/45R18 ×4): rotate every ~5,000–7,500 miles.** Even wear is the
   best transfer-case insurance you can buy, and it's usually free with tire purchase.
6. **Staggered setup (19" 225/40 F + 255/35 R): no front↔rear rotation.** The sizes are engineered
   to match (≈26.09" vs 26.03" diameter, ~0.2% apart). Side-to-side only, if non-directional.
   The 791M wheels also have different widths/offsets front vs rear.
7. **Staggered sets wear rears faster** and some makers **halve the treadwear warranty on rears**
   (Goodyear states this for the Eagle F1 All Season). Budget for it.

## Symptoms that it's already happening
Jerk or shudder when **turning at low speed** or **accelerating gently from a stop**, usually with
**no warning light**. If you have it: check tire wear/sizes first; BMW's fix after that is a
**transfer-case fluid change** (see [`/maintenance/transfer-case-and-differentials.md`](../maintenance/transfer-case-and-differentials.md)).

## What BMW's bulletin (SIB 27 02 20, rev. 5, Jun 2025; covers G20) tells technicians
- Check tire **size and brand** against the B-pillar placard.
- "One or more tires with **extreme tire wear will keep the xDrive engaged**."
- If tires were just replaced, the damage may have happened on the old tires.
- On staggered cars, make sure fronts haven't been swapped to the rear.
- Measuring **rolling circumference of all four** is "highly recommended."
- Tire specs must be documented for warranty approval, so mismatched tires can sink a claim.
- If tires are fine: diagnose, then **change transfer-case oil** (DTF-1, ~0.7 L).
