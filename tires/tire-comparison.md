# Tire comparison: specific models, prices, and test results

**Applies to:** G20 330i / 330i xDrive in 225/45R18 (square) and 225/40R19 + 255/35R19 (staggered)

> **TL;DR / ELI5:** For **one set, all year, in a moderate-snow climate**: on **18"** the
> **Pirelli P Zero AS Plus 3** won Tire Rack's test *on this exact car* (quietest, liveliest steering);
> the **Michelin Pilot Sport All Season 4** grips hardest but is noisier. On **19"** the **Continental
> DWS06 Plus** is the forum favorite with the best snow record, and the new **Goodyear Eagle F1 All
> Season** won Tire Rack's 2026 test on a 330i xDrive. For **summer-only**, the **Michelin Pilot
> Sport 4S** is the benchmark; the **Goodyear Eagle F1 Asymmetric 6** is better in rain and a bit
> cheaper.

## The tests behind the numbers
**UHP all-season, on a 330i xDrive (the most relevant data that exists):**
- **Tire Rack 2024 "Search for Balance"** (2024 330i xDrive): P Zero AS+3 **7.89** (best wet,
  quietest, lively steering, *lower winter traction*) · DWS06+ 7.37 · G-MAX AS-07 ~7.37 · PSAS4 7.23
  (highest measured grip, noisiest, "very real cost" in winter) · Falken FK460 7.12 · Potenza Sport AS 7.02.
- **Tire Rack 2026** (330i xDrive, 235/40R19, published Jul 9 2026): **Eagle F1 All Season** won (road
  score 8.5, comfort 7.8, noise 7.5, quickest dry lap) · DWS06+ 2nd (shortest wet braking) · PSAS4 3rd
  (shortest stops, most grip, noisiest) · Potenza Sport AS 4th. **Winter results due early 2027.**
- AutoView 2025 (BMW 520i): P Zero AS+3 1st, PSAS4 2nd, DWS06+ 3rd.
- Consumer Reports 2026: top UHP all-season PSAS4, runner-up DWS06+.
- Bimmerpost consensus: **DWS06+ most recommended**; AS+3 very quiet, weaker in snow.

**Summer:**
- Tire Rack 2025 (BMW M2): ECS02 best dry handling; **Asymmetric 6 best wet handling and dry
  braking**; Kumho best ride/noise; PS4S showed some front "chattering understeer" at the limit.
- Tire Rack "Best Summer Tires of 2026": Asymmetric 6 = top wet-weather pick.
- Consumer Reports 2026: PS4S top UHP summer (highest score of any tire tested), ECS02 runner-up.
  CR Sept 2026: budget Sumitomo HTR Z5 / Vredestein Ultrac Pro grip almost as well; premium tires win
  on noise and owner satisfaction.
- PS5 vs PS4S: PS5 quieter/more supple; PS4S more dry grip. Potenza Sport vs PS4S: sharper and better
  in wet, but harsher and louder.

**All-weather / winter:** CR ranks Quatrac Pro+ #1 all-weather; CrossClimate 2 best in snow/ice.
Hakkapeliitta R5 beat X-Ice Snow in 6 of 8 shared tests; Pilot Alpin 5 far better in wet, X-Ice
better on ice.

## Picks by driver
| Driver | #1 | Runner-up | How it'll feel |
|---|---|---|---|
| Enthusiast, warm/dry climate | **Michelin PS4S** | Goodyear Asymmetric 6 (better rain, ~$40/tire less) | Steering weights up, crisp turn-in, glued mid-corner, much shorter stops. Firmer over sharp bumps. **Not below ~40°F** |
| One set, 4 seasons, **18" square** | **Pirelli P Zero AS Plus 3** (95 XL) | Michelin PSAS4 (more grip, louder) · **Quatrac Pro+** if real snow is a regular thing | ~85% of summer sharpness, quiet, composed in rain, fine in light/moderate snow with xDrive |
| One set, 4 seasons, **19" staggered** | **Continental DWS06 Plus** | Goodyear Eagle F1 All Season | Same, with the best-proven snow ability of the UHP group |
| Comfort / commuter | **Pirelli P Zero AS Plus 3** (non-run-flat) | Goodyear Eagle F1 All Season | Expansion joints become soft thumps, highway noise drops, steering a bit lighter but accurate |
| Snow belt, two sets | Summer: **PS4S** / Winter on 18": **X-Ice Snow** or **Hakkapeliitta R5** | Asymmetric 6 / Pilot Alpin 5 | Winter set: soft on dry, unflappable on snow and ice |
