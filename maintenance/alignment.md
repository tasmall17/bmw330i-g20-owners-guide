# Wheel alignment

**Applies to:** 2019–2024 G20 330i & 330i xDrive · **Category:** maintenance

> **TL;DR / ELI5:** Alignment is setting the angles the wheels point at, so the car tracks straight and the
> tires wear evenly. It isn't a scheduled service. You need it when something changes: new suspension or
> steering parts, a hard pothole/curb hit, the car pulls, the steering wheel is off-center, or tires are
> wearing on one edge. Dealers often recommend it with every set of tires. That's reasonable insurance on
> $1,000+ of tires but not mandatory. Bottom line: **when you buy tires, at least get an alignment *check*
> with a printout; pay for a full alignment if anything is out of spec or the old tires wore unevenly.**

## How much it actually matters: **3/5**
Misalignment quietly eats expensive tires (especially run-flats). But a car that tracks straight and wears
evenly doesn't need one just because.

## What the forums say
- **"Only with symptoms" camp:** align after suspension/steering/bushing work, a curb hit, a pull, an off-center
  wheel, or uneven wear. A BMW CCA columnist says a ~0.2° side-to-side camber difference isn't worth chasing
  if the car tracks straight and wear is even.
- **"Every tire set" camp (dealers, tire shops):** protect the new tires; it's cheap relative to the set.
- **Where consensus lands:** **alignment check with a before/after printout** when buying tires; full alignment
  only if out of spec or the old tires showed uneven wear. Always after suspension work.

## When to do it
| Source | Interval |
|---|---|
| BMW | No scheduled interval. Excluded from Ultimate Care; covered only in the first 2,000 mi of the new-car warranty |
| Enthusiast / forum consensus | After suspension/steering work, impacts, or with new tires *if* the check shows it's out |
| Signs it's due now | Pulls to one side, off-center steering wheel, **inner-edge wear** (often rear toe), feathered tread, after replacing control arms/thrust arms/tie rods |

## DIY verdict: **Leave it to a shop**
- **You need:** a shop with an alignment rack loaded with BMW specs, and a steering-angle sensor reset afterward.
- **The gotcha:** BMW specifies aligning with the car **loaded to a set ballast** (weight in the front seats and
  trunk, full tank) ⚠️ unverified detail. Few quick-lube shops do this. On the G20 **rear toe is adjustable;
  rear camber isn't** (forum-reported factory rear toe spec ≈ −0.08° to +0.08°). Whether front camber is
  adjustable without parts ⚠️ unverified. A driver-assist camera recalibration (KAFAS) is required if the
  camera was disturbed; whether it's needed after a plain alignment is debated.

## Watch out for
- **Alignment sold on every visit or every rotation** = upsell unless there's a symptom.
- **Always ask for the before/after printout.** If "before" was in spec, you paid for a check, not a fix.
- **Red-flag threshold:** over **$300** for a four-wheel alignment.
- With new tires on xDrive, matched tires matter more than alignment for the transfer case: see
  [`../tires/xdrive-tire-rules.md`](../tires/xdrive-tire-rules.md). Rotation: [`tire-rotation-and-balance.md`](tire-rotation-and-balance.md).
