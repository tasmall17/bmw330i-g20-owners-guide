# Cabin air filter (microfilter)

**Applies to:** 2019–2024 G20 330i & 330i xDrive · **Category:** maintenance

> **TL;DR / ELI5:** This is the air filter for *you*, not the engine. It cleans the air coming through
> the vents and has activated charcoal to soak up smells. When it clogs, airflow drops, the windows
> fog more easily, and the cabin can smell musty. It does nothing for the engine's health. The G20 has
> **one** filter, behind the glovebox, and swapping it is about a 10-minute job. **Bottom line: replace it
> when the car asks (~every 20k miles / 2 years), and do it yourself for about $30–85. The dealer
> charges about $250 for the same thing.**

## How much it actually matters: **2/5**
Comfort and air quality, plus less strain on the blower. Nothing breaks if it's late.

## What the forums say
- **Nobody argues to skip the filter.** The debate is the **dealer labor** for a no-tools job.
- **Common view on Bimmerpost G20 forum:** about 10 minutes once you know it; refitting the access
  panel can take ~30 minutes the first time. MANN filters are the OEM part without the BMW logo.
- The G20 has **one** filter. A dealer menu line saying "may require recirc filter" is a generic template.
- **Where consensus lands:** DIY it every 1–2 years, sooner with heavy pollen, dust, or smells.

## When to do it
| Source | Interval |
|---|---|
| BMW (CBS / 2021 booklet) | Every **2nd oil service** (~20k mi / ~2 yrs) |
| Enthusiast / forum consensus | Yearly to 2 years; sooner with allergies, dusty roads, or odors |
| Signs it's due now | Weak airflow at high fan, musty smell, windows slow to defog |

## DIY verdict: **Dead simple** (with one important gotcha)
- **You need:** filter **64 11 9 382 886** (activated charcoal, ×1), a flashlight. Access is in the
  **passenger footwell, behind the cover under the glovebox**. Specs:
  [`../vehicle/specs-and-fluids.md`](../vehicle/specs-and-fluids.md).
- **The gotcha:** BMW **SIB 64 03 21** (Jun 2021, lists the G20) says the fresh-air/recirculation flap
  can be **pushed past its end stop** while you slide the filter in. You get weak airflow and outside
  smells, and the fix means removing the blower. **BMW's instruction: switch recirculation OFF
  (fresh-air mode, LED off) before inserting the filter.** A popular Bimmerpost "Warning!!!" thread
  says turn recirculation **ON**. **Follow the SIB, not the forum.**
- Note the airflow arrow on the filter. No CBS reset is strictly needed (reset with an app if you want
  the reminder cleared).

## Watch out for
- Dealer labor of 0.5+ hours for this job is the classic upsell. See [`../where-to-service/upsells.md`](../where-to-service/upsells.md).
- **"A/C deodorizing" / evaporator cleaning** sold alongside: usually a new charcoal filter fixes the
  smell. Try that first.
- If airflow is weak *after* a filter change (yours or a shop's), ask about SIB 64 03 21.
