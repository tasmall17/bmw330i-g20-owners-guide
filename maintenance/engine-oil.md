# Engine oil & filter

**Applies to:** 2019–2024 G20 330i & 330i xDrive (B46/B48) · **Category:** maintenance

> **TL;DR / ELI5:** Oil is the engine's blood. It cools, cleans, and keeps metal from touching
> metal in the turbo, the timing chain, and the bearings. It wears out with miles *and* time, and a
> direct-injected turbo engine like this one dirties it faster (fuel sneaks into it, especially on
> short trips). BMW's car tells you when it's due (about every 10,000 miles or once a year). Many
> owners change it sooner, every 5,000–7,500 miles. It's the cheapest protection you can buy for the
> engine. **Bottom line: never skip it. Do it at least when the car asks, and consider 5–7.5k if you
> do lots of short trips or keep the car long-term.**

## How much it actually matters: **5/5**
Oil is cheap and turbos, timing chains, and bearings aren't. Stretching oil on a direct-injected turbo is
the most common self-inflicted wear on these engines.

## What the forums say
- **"BMW's interval is fine" camp:** common view on the Bimmerpost G20 forum: BMW-approved oil handles
  10k miles of normal street driving; 15k+ is pushing it. CBS shortens the interval for hard use.
  BMW itself already *cut* the interval to 1 yr / 10k on other engines in 2019 (SIB 01 24 18), so
  10k/1 yr is BMW's corrected number, not the old 15k "long-life" figure.
- **"Change it sooner" camp:** many G20 owners change around 5k. Bob Is The Oil Guy B48/MINI threads
  suggest 5–7.5k. BimmerWorld: "Our oil change recommendation is 5,000 miles." Reasons: fuel dilution
  on a direct-injected turbo, short trips, and keeping timing-chain oil clean.
- **Where consensus lands:** enthusiasts do **5k–7.5k or yearly**. 10k/1 yr is defensible for mostly
  highway miles on approved oil. A used-oil analysis (~$35) settles it for *your* car.
- **0W-20 vs 0W-30:** US cars specify **LL-17 FE+ 0W-20** (fuel-economy rules); other markets get
  0W-30. Stay on whatever viscosity/approval is on your manual's list. If you move to 0W-30 out of
  warranty, choose one that is BMW-approved *and* API SP.

## When to do it
| Source | Interval |
|---|---|
| BMW (CBS) | ~10,000 mi or 1 year, whichever first; CBS shortens it for hard use |
| Enthusiast / forum consensus | 5,000–7,500 mi or yearly |
| Signs it's due now | CBS "Engine oil" due, oil-level warning, lots of short trips/towing/track since last change |

## DIY verdict: **Doable with tools**
- **You need:** BMW **Longlife-17 FE+ 0W-20** (e.g. BMW TwinPower, Castrol EDGE LL-17FE+, Liqui Moly
  Top Tec 6600, Motul 8100 Eco-Clean). Filter kit **11 42 8 575 211** (element, cap O-ring, drain
  washer). ⚠️ **Not 11 42 8 583 898**: that's the B58 six-cylinder filter. 36 mm filter-cap socket,
  drain plug socket, torque wrench (drain plug and cap 25 Nm / ~18 ft-lb), and a
  BMW-capable scan app (BimmerLink, Carly) or tool to reset CBS. Full specs:
  [`../vehicle/specs-and-fluids.md`](../vehicle/specs-and-fluids.md).
- **The gotcha:** **there's no dipstick.** Capacity is ~5.25 L (sources range to 5.75 L). Fill
  **~5.0 L**, run the engine warm, then use iDrive's electronic oil-level measurement (car level) and
  top up only to what it says. The undertray has to come off; the filter is on top of the engine.
- **CBS reset:** pre-LCI cars have an instrument-cluster button procedure (⚠️ unverified; timing is
  fiddly on iDrive 8). Most owners just use BimmerLink.

## Watch out for
- **Prepaid "oil plans."** Some dealers sell a 3-year oil-service plan (seen at ~$249 in 2026). Math vs.
  Value Service at $119.99/visit: **2 visits = $240, 3 visits = $360.** So a ~$249 plan **breaks even at
  ~2.1 dealer oil services** and pays off at 3+ within the 3 years. CBS asks roughly once a year, so 3
  services in 3 years is typical. It only wins if: you'll keep the car 3 years, you'll service at that
  dealer, and the plan covers CBS-scheduled changes (extra 5k changes usually aren't covered). Ask
  whether it transfers or refunds if you sell.
- **"Engine flush"** with an oil change: fluff, with some risk. LL-17 FE+ oils already carry the
  detergents. See [`../where-to-service/upsells.md`](../where-to-service/upsells.md).
- Keep receipts (incl. DIY oil/filter receipts): they protect emissions-warranty claims
  ([`../vehicle/warranty-and-coverage.md`](../vehicle/warranty-and-coverage.md)).
