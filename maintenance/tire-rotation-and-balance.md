# Tire rotation & balance

**Applies to:** 2019–2024 G20 330i & 330i xDrive; rotation rules depend on **square vs staggered** wheels · **Category:** maintenance

> **TL;DR / ELI5:** Rotating means moving tires to different corners so they wear evenly; balancing means
> adding small weights so a wheel spins without vibrating. If all four of your tires are the **same size**
> ("square," e.g. 225/45R18 ×4) you can and should rotate front↔back every ~5,000–7,500 miles. On an
> **xDrive** car that's extra important, because even wear keeps all four tires the same size, which protects
> the expensive transfer case. If your car has **different sizes front and rear** ("staggered," e.g.
> 19" M Sport wheels), you can't rotate front↔back. Bottom line: **square + xDrive → rotate every 5–7.5k;
> balance only when mounting tires or if you feel a vibration.**

## How much it actually matters: **3/5** (**4/5 on xDrive**)
Even wear stretches tire life. On xDrive it also keeps rolling diameters matched; BMW's own shudder bulletin
lists unevenly worn tires as a cause.

## What the forums say
- **"Don't bother" camp:** some BMW owners skip rotation because negative camber creates edge-wear patterns and
  rotating can add noise *(common view ⚠️ unverified forum citation)*.
- **"Do it" camp:** on xDrive, rotation keeps all four tires the same diameter, protecting the transfer case.
- **Where consensus lands:** rotate **square xDrive cars every 5–7.5k**; on xDrive replace all four tires
  together (or shave one to match). **Balance** only at tire mounting or for a vibration.

## When to do it
| Your setup | Rule |
|---|---|
| **Square** (same size ×4, e.g. 18" Style 780 225/45R18) | Rotate **front↔rear every ~5,000–7,500 mi** |
| **Staggered** (e.g. 19" 791M: 225/40R19 F / 255/35R19 R) | **No front↔rear rotation.** Side-to-side only, and only with non-directional tires (needs remounting; the 791M wheels also differ in width/offset front vs rear) |
| Balance | When tires are mounted; when a vibration appears (often 60–75 mph); after a lost wheel weight |
| Signs it's due now | Front and rear tread depths drifting apart by more than ~2/32"; vibration |

Not sure which you have? Read the sidewalls or see [`../tires/oe-fitments.md`](../tires/oe-fitments.md).

## DIY verdict: **Doable with tools**
- **You need:** a low-profile jack, **BMW jack-pad (puck) adapters**, jack stands, a torque wrench.
- **The gotcha:** torque the wheel bolts to spec (BMW **140 Nm / ~103 lb-ft**) by hand. Then **set pressures to
  the placard** (they often differ front vs rear) and do a **TPMS reset** in iDrive (Vehicle status → Tire
  Pressure Monitor → Perform reset), and drive.

## Watch out for
- **"Road-force balance" upsell:** worth it on 19" wheels if you actually feel a vibration; not needed by default.
- **Nitrogen fill** bundled with rotation = upsell. Checking pressure monthly matters more.
- **xDrive:** if one tire gets ruined, don't replace just one or two. Match tread depth (shave a new tire to
  match). See [`../tires/xdrive-tire-rules.md`](../tires/xdrive-tire-rules.md).
- **Red-flag threshold:** paying dealer rotation prices on tires that came with free lifetime rotations elsewhere.
