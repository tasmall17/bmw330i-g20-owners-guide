# BMW "Vehicle Check" (and multi-point inspections)

**Applies to:** 2019–2024 G20 330i & 330i xDrive · **Category:** maintenance (inspection)

> **TL;DR / ELI5:** "Vehicle Check" is a CBS item that's an **inspection, not a repair**: a technician
> looks over lights, belts, tires, brakes, underbody, leaks, scans for faults and tests the battery,
> then resets the reminder. No parts get replaced. Under BMW's free maintenance (first 3 yrs / 36k) it
> costs nothing. After that it's essentially a multi-point inspection, which dealers often do free with
> other service anyway. It's useful as a set of trained eyes, especially if nobody ever looks under your
> car. **Bottom line: worth having done, but don't pay $150–300 for it on its own. Get it bundled with an
> oil service, or do your own look-over and reset the reminder.**

## How much it actually matters: **2/5**
Valuable only as early detection (leaks, worn bushings, tire wear). The check itself doesn't maintain anything.

## What the forums say
- **"Glorified multi-point inspection" camp:** out of warranty, forum opinion calls it a free MPI
  with a price tag; DIYers inspect the car and reset CBS themselves.
- **"Worth it" camp:** a lift and a trained eye catch seeps, cracked mounts, and bushing wear that
  owners can't see from the driveway.
- **Where consensus lands:** get it when it's free or bundled; skip it as a paid stand-alone line.

## When to do it
| Source | Interval |
|---|---|
| BMW (CBS) | Time- and mileage-based; often shows ~2–3 yrs / 30–40k (⚠️ varies by car) |
| Enthusiast / forum consensus | At least once a year, ideally on a lift (bundled with oil service) |
| Signs it's due now | CBS "Vehicle check" due; any new noise, smell, drip, or warning light |

## DIY verdict: **Dead simple**
- **You need:** flashlight, tread-depth gauge, tire-pressure gauge, a scan app (BimmerLink/Carly) to
  reset the CBS item.
- **Checklist:** all lights · wiper/washer · tire tread (all four, inner edges too) and pressures · look
  under the engine for oil/coolant drips · oil level via iDrive · coolant level (cold) · brake pad
  CBS readings · listen for clunks over bumps.
- **The gotcha:** you can't see the underbody well without a lift. Ask a shop to do it with an oil change.

## Watch out for
- **The MPI is a sales tool as well as an inspection.** Treat recommendations as a list to verify, not
  a list to approve. Ask for **measurements** (pad thickness in mm, tread depth in 32nds), photos or
  video, and the **CBS due/not-due** status for each item.
- **Fuel filter** on an MPI: it's a lifetime in-tank part on this car. N/A, not a sale.
- Items marked "Caution" (e.g. tires at 4–5/32") are **plan-ahead** items, not emergencies.
