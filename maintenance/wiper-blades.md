# Wiper blades

**Applies to:** 2019–2024 G20 330i & 330i xDrive · **Category:** maintenance (wear item)

> **TL;DR / ELI5:** Wiper rubber hardens and cracks from sun and cold. When the blades streak,
> chatter, or leave a haze, you can't see well in rain or at night. They cost $20–75 a pair and take
> five minutes to swap. The only trick on a BMW is putting the wipers in "service position" first so
> the arms clear the hood. **Bottom line: replace them yearly or when they streak, and do it yourself.**

## How much it actually matters: **2/5**
A visibility and safety item, but cheap and trivial.

## What the forums say
- **Nobody debates replacing them.** The only argument is paying dealer install labor for a no-tools job.
- **Where consensus lands:** replace every year or when they streak/chatter. Many owners do it each fall
  before winter.

## When to do it
| Source | Interval |
|---|---|
| BMW | Wear item, not covered by Ultimate Care; inspected during Vehicle Check |
| Enthusiast / forum consensus | Yearly, or at first streaking/chatter |
| Signs it's due now | Streaks, haze, chatter, torn or stiff rubber, split edges |

## DIY verdict: **Dead simple**
- **You need:** 24" driver and 19" passenger blades (BMW-fit connector). Specs:
  [`../vehicle/specs-and-fluids.md`](../vehicle/specs-and-fluids.md).
- **The gotcha:** put the wipers in **service position** first, or the arms can't lift clear of the
  hood edge. Common method: ignition on then off, then hold the wiper stalk up ~3 seconds
  (⚠️ unverified for every software version; check your owner's manual). Lower the arms gently
  so they don't snap onto the glass.

## Watch out for
- Dealer labor on a 5-minute job; buy blades online or at a parts store.
- Cheap no-name blades that don't fit the BMW connector cleanly.
