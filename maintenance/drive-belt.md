# Drive (serpentine) belt & tensioner

**Applies to:** 2019–2024 G20 330i & 330i xDrive (B46/B48) · **Category:** maintenance

> **TL;DR / ELI5:** The drive belt is the rubber belt on the front of the engine that spins accessories like
> the alternator and A/C compressor (and possibly the main coolant pump). A spring-loaded tensioner keeps it
> tight. Belts slowly crack and stretch; if one snaps, you lose those accessories, and debris can get pulled
> into the front of the engine. There's no BMW interval. Bottom line: **have it looked at from ~60k miles and
> replace it (ideally with the tensioner/idler) around 80–100k, or sooner if it's cracked or chirping.**

## How much it actually matters: **2/5** (→ **3/5** if your coolant pump is belt-driven)
Cheap, slow-wearing, and easy to inspect. More important if the belt turns the main coolant pump, since a
snapped belt would then mean overheating. ⚠️ See the pump note in [`coolant.md`](coolant.md).

## What the forums say
- **"Wait for symptoms" camp:** no fixed interval from BMW; replace on cracks, glazing, fraying, or chirp
  *(common view ⚠️ unverified forum citation)*.
- **"Do it preventively" camp:** inspect from ~60k; replace belt **plus tensioner and idler as a kit** around
  80–100k, especially on a car you're keeping.
- **Where consensus lands:** inspection is cheap (part of any decent multi-point check); preventive kit
  replacement ~80–100k.

## When to do it
| Source | Interval |
|---|---|
| BMW | **No fixed interval**: inspection item; excluded from BMW Ultimate Care |
| Enthusiast / forum consensus | Inspect from ~60k; replace ~80–100k, belt + tensioner/idler together |
| Signs it's due now | Cracks across the ribs, fraying edges, glazing, chirp/squeal on cold start, tensioner arm bouncing, oil-soaked belt (find the leak too) |

## DIY verdict: **Doable with tools**
- **You need:** the correct belt for your VIN (RealOEM), a **tensioner release tool** (or long-handle wrench),
  and a **photo of the routing before you remove it.**
- **The gotcha:** access details on the G20 are tight ⚠️ unverified. Release the tensioner slowly. A snapping
  tensioner can catch fingers. **An oil-soaked belt means an upstream oil leak** (oil filter housing / valve
  cover). Fix that first or the new belt dies too.

## Watch out for
- **Oil on the belt is the expensive scenario** with this engine: a leaking oil filter housing gasket can drip
  onto the belt, which can then fail and get ingested. See [`../known-issues/`](../known-issues/README.md).
- A belt "recommended" at a low mileage with no visible cracks is worth a second opinion. Ask to see it.
- Drive belts are wear items and excluded from BMW Ultimate Care.
